#### Jumpscaring

Recorded Moment allows Joseph to enter or exit the Camera World in any locations without relying on the fixed camera positions – it’s a great way for him to jumpscare survivors on ciphers, chests, or other actions that reveal their location through sound cues (such as when Mechanic is using her robot or when they’re placing graffitis in the other world).

However, using Recorded Moment to terrorshock is not very reliable, as some survivors have good ping and fast reflexes on top of Joseph’s very slow attack startup time (0.6s). Survivors with good game sense will also be more likely to anticipate and avoid it. They may also feel safe when Joseph is ballooning a mirror image, since the balloon dropping animation adds to the total accumulated time.

A better method of terrorshocking someone on cipher is to drop down the ballooned mirror image near a camera projection and immediately swing through it without using Recorded Moment. While this relies on good camera placements near ciphers, this doesn’t allow survivors a lot of time to react.
